Work / 06 Where I started

The calculator
was unnecessary.

A battery-powered 36-key soundboard with a VFD, stereo speakers and a macro-pad mode. It was the first board I finished, and the project where electronics, firmware and enclosure design first had to cooperate.

Year
2021
Role
Hardware, enclosure and firmware
Modes
Soundboard · macro-pad · calculator
Licence
CERN-OHL-S v2.0

01What it is

At its simplest, press a key and Schroombort plays a sound from the SD card. Underneath are 36 mechanical switches, a VFD, stereo speakers, USB-C charging and a battery power-path manager that can run from USB, battery or both.

Connected over USB-C, it becomes a macro-pad with assignable keys. The third mode is a basic calculator. The display and keypad were already there; restraint was not.

03The first finished board

This was the first project where power management, a keyboard matrix, display driving, audio, an enclosure and firmware all had to work at once. It also exposed the difference between readable on a monitor and readable on an actual PCB.

It was over-engineered for a soundboard. Finishing one complete object still taught me more than another row of breadboard experiments would have.
