Work / 06 Where I started

The board that
started it.

Schroombort is a battery-powered soundboard with 36 Cherry MX switches, a vacuum fluorescent display and stereo speakers. It also works as a macro-pad and a basic calculator. It was the first board I finished, and many of the skills used in the later projects started here.

Year
2021
Role
Hardware, enclosure and firmware
Modes

Licence
CERN-OHL-S v2.0

01What it is

At its simplest, Schroombort plays a sound when you press a key. It has 36 mechanical switches, a VFD, stereo speakers, USB-C charging and a battery power-path manager that can run from USB, battery or both.

It has three modes. As a soundboard, each key plays a file from the SD card. Connected over USB-C, it becomes a macro-pad with assignable keys. The third mode is a basic calculator, added because the hardware already had a display and keypad.

03Why it matters

This was the project that taught me what it takes to finish a piece of hardware: power management, a keyboard matrix, display driving, audio, an enclosure and firmware all have to work together. It also taught me that details which look fine on screen do not always read well on a physical board.

It was more complex than the original idea required, but that made it a useful way to learn. Later projects could build on a complete design rather than a collection of separate experiments.
